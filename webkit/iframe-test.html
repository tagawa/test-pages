WebKit iFrame Test

Creates many iframes in a short burst, as some ad, video and carousel scripts do. Load this page with ?resume=1&mode=blank to start. It starts again on every load, including after an unexpected reload. Other modes: churn (each frame removed after 0.5 s), srcdoc and url. Add &count= to change the number of frames (default 400) and &interval= to change the delay between frames in ms (default 20).
